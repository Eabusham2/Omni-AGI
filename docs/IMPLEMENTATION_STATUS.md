## Deferred native qualification

No new brain has been created or trained for this source audit. Fluency,
parameter-only recall after unrelated conversation/Fresh/restart, full-corpus
competence, acoustic/media quality, useful natural tool choice and beneficial
recursive improvement require an explicitly authorized trained-native run.
Brain-inspired mechanisms are not claims of consciousness, biological
equivalence, guaranteed AGI or perfect memory.
