# Same-brain voice connection and quality boundary

Platform STT and TTS remain the defaults. The optional own-voice output and the
compact saved-reply playback action use the selected brain's native audio region.
They do not invoke platform TTS, a pretrained speech model, or an external speech
service. A new mind can produce an untrained waveform; this is not evidence that
the waveform contains intelligible speech.

The renderer sends the literal reply through `modality.generateSpeech`. Main
creates an independently cancellable audio job, and the worker routes it to the
existing text-conditioned `brain.generate_modality(audio)` graph. Both main and
playback verify brain identity, request identity, and the exact UTF-8 text SHA-256.
Only the owned generated WAV or its trusted artifact lease is played. Late jobs
and stale completions cannot start cancelled playback. Producing audio is separate
from active text generation, and cancellation does not revoke the saved reply.

UI readiness distinguishes an available audio region from trained perception and
from verified speech. With no paired examples it says “Needs speech training.”
After paired examples it says “Speech quality not verified.”
`intelligibilityVerified` remains false in either case.

## Explicit paired-speech ingest

Local JSONL rows (or records in a JSON array) may declare:

```json
{"format":"omni-speech-pair-1","audioPath":"clip.wav","audioSha256":"<64 hexadecimal characters>","text":"literal recorded utterance"}
```

The clip must resolve inside the source directory. Its declared checksum is
verified, and the record identity binds both clip and literal transcript hashes.
No transcript is inferred from filenames, generated by ASR, replayed as a stored
answer, or silently shortened. Large transcript materialization is subject to
measured physical RAM admission because the present text-to-idea library still
requires one utterance.

Paired audio is normalized from its measured sample rate to the actual 16 kHz
voice route, using bounded phase-continuous PCM resampling. PCM WAV or a supported
soundfile input must expose its real rate. The training path uses the same
`_modality_idea(text)` conditioning as output and adds a waveform loss directly on
the native audio generator, in addition to its existing reconstruction loss.
Padding is excluded from speech targets. Generator supervision is admitted for
the additional native graphs, and paired-example counters persist with neural
metadata. Generic audio training alone does not increment that counter.

Constructor-free transport, parser, player and primitive loss/resampling fixtures
cover these connections. They do not verify acoustic quality, pronunciation,
word alignment, intelligibility, or long-utterance continuity. Those remain live
trained-model acceptance gates; no app, live audio, training or full brain/model
constructor was run for this integration.
